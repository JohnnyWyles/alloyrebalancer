Alloy Rebalancer

Takes one variant out of an Osmosis alloy and puts another back in, bridging between them so the alloy's composition shifts while your alloy balance stays the same, minus gas and bridge fees. Not an arbitrage: the transmuter swaps 1:1 with no fee. Routes exist for Alloyed USDC, USDT, BTC and ETH; Alloyed SOL and XRP are composition view only.
Alpha, unaudited. Every route is verified against decoded payloads and reference implementations, but no route has yet been exercised end to end with a live wallet by anyone but you. Use small amounts first. Routes through Nomic, Chainflip or Injective's Peggy bridge are marked experimental, capped, and need an explicit acknowledgement before they will start.
